# NEXUS OS

Sources: https://www.rutinhq.com/nexus-os · https://docs.rutinhq.com/catalog/nexus-os/

Agentic marketing — Strategy → Social → Ads. Until creatives, mix, and budget have an explicit GO: paper only (no pixel, no spend). Produces content batches and plans; does not invent the retailer’s offer or ICP.

For whom: Retailer / ecom with a clear offer and SKUs; wants demand from social as a system; brand voice owned by the client’s ops/marketing.

Not for: No offer / SKU; ads with zero creative bank; budget 0 pretending to be “live Ads”.

How: 1) Brief + content pillars (one problem, one object). 2) Weekly paper batch: message · organic post · product story. 3) Voice approval by the client’s brand owner. 4) Pillar rotation. 5) Ads parked until photos + mix + budget are signed.

Outcomes: repeatable content system without invented claims; paper ready for review before spend; clear split vs GTM OS (outbound) and STORE OS (Admin).

What it is not: live Ads / pixel / spend without GO; owner of the client’s ICP or offer; GTM OS; Shopify theme editing.
