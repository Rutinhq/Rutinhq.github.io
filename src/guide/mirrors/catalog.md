# Systems catalog

Source: https://docs.rutinhq.com/catalog/

Public curriculum of systems RutinHQ designs and installs — what they do, how they work, who they are for, and what outcome they chase. Not an ops wiki. One sheet = one system.

Phase-1 sheets:

- gtm-os — GTM OS — B2B outbound that stays with the client
- store-os — STORE OS — Shopify Admin audit + config so the store converts
- nexus-os — NEXUS OS — Agentic marketing (Strategy → Social → Ads gated)
- grok-bot-os — GROK BOT OS — bot-department curriculum (not a commercial hub card)

You will not find here: client names; pricing / live rate cards; day-of state; inboxes; secrets; lead lists.
