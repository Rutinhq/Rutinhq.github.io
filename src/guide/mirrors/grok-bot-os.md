# GROK BOT OS (catalog curriculum)

Source: https://docs.rutinhq.com/catalog/grok-bot-os/

Public catalog sheet only. Not sold as a fourth card on www.rutinhq.com. The commercial products remain GTM OS, STORE OS, and NEXUS OS.

High-level: an operating-system design for scoped agents (charter → staged writes → explicit promote). Weekly state, keys, and runbooks do not live on the public DocuSite.

This sheet is not a price catalog and not a dump of system prompts. If a visitor asks to buy “a bot OS”, steer to the three commercial SKUs or a 30-min fit call.
